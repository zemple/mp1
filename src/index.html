01 · Habitat

Built by ice,
shaped by light.

The Arctic is not one empty expanse. Sea ice, tundra, coastal cliffs, and open water form a connected living system.

Each season redraws the map. Winter locks the ocean in ice; summer opens narrow passages and brings a rush of migrating life.
